# Version 5 English store draft

Amazon distribution and scoped version 5 device checks passed. Prepared public draft copy; public submission remains blocked by the remaining release gates.

**Title:** LifeTalea - Family Stories & Voice Journal

**Short description:** Keep family stories, photos and voice recordings together. Find memories with useful filters and review on-device transcript drafts.

**Long description:**

Keep the moments. Remember the voices.

Give family stories a place on your Fire tablet. Begin with a photograph, a few written lines or a voice recording. Add tags, an optional place and a date as precisely as you know it. Organize albums, mark favourites and explore a family timeline.

Find a chapter again with text, year, album, favourites, photo, audio and transcript filters. Clear filters to return to the full library. Gather memories into scrapbook drafts and add captions.

Create an on-device transcript draft from a saved recording. Choose its spoken language, confirm local processing, then review and correct the words before appending them. No audio is uploaded for transcription. Recordings up to ten minutes can be processed; processing time and accuracy vary. Original audio and manual editing remain available.

Turn on optional local reminders for a generic daily prompt around 20:00 local time. Device settings can delay notifications. Reminders are off by default and contain no story content.

No LifeTalea account is required. The journal stays on your device. Choose what to include in local backups. Speech commands and spoken playback depend on installed speech services; touch and text alternatives remain available. Settings explains purchase verification and links to the full privacy notice.

Optional Premium is a one-time purchase for unlimited albums, longer recordings, keepsake PDF exports and encrypted local backups. Amazon shows the local price before purchase. Existing memories remain accessible without Premium. Restore uses the Amazon account registered on the device.

**Feature bullets:**

- Keep stories, photographs and voice recordings together.
- Find memories with combined year, album and media filters.
- Review and correct on-device transcript drafts before saving.
- Choose optional local reminders without showing journal content.
- Create scrapbook drafts and local backups you control.
