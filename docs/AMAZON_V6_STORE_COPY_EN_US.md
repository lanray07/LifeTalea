LifeTalea - Family stories and voice memories

A private place for photographs, voices and family stories.

Keep the moments. Remember the voices.

A private place for photographs, voices and family stories.

Write a title and a few lines. Add an exact date, an approximate year or no date at all. Tags, favorites and albums help you find the story again.

Import photographs using your device’s document picker. Record a voice story and keep it beside the written memory. Media is copied into private app storage.

Audio stays on this device. Leaving the app stops and saves this recording. Free recordings are limited to three minutes.

Create a draft from this recording on your device. No audio is uploaded. Choose the spoken language, then confirm. Recordings up to ten minutes are supported. Processing may be slow; check the draft for mistakes before saving.

Push to talk uses your device’s speech service, which may need internet and process speech externally. Only continue if you agree. Audio commands are not saved by LifeTalea. Command phrases currently support English.

One purchase for unlimited albums, longer recordings, keepsake PDF exports and encrypted local backups. Your existing memories always stay accessible.

Stories, transcripts, albums and scrapbook drafts are included. Photos and recordings are included only when selected. Unencrypted exports contain readable private information. Keep your passphrase safe; it cannot be recovered. Current archive limits: 32 MiB total and 16 MiB per attachment. Larger libraries need a future streaming backup implementation.

Amazon purchases send app-specific Amazon user and receipt identifiers to LifeTalea’s Supabase-hosted verifier and Amazon. Providers may retain request and connection logs. Stories, photos and recordings are not sent for verification. Saved-recording transcription runs on this device. Exported files must be deleted separately.

Optional daily reminder around 20:00 local time. Delivery may be delayed by device settings. Notifications contain no story content. Off by default.

Use your device screen lock to unlock LifeTalea after leaving the app. Set up a device screen lock first. Exported files are separate.
