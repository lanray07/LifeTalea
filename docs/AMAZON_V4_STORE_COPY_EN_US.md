# Version 4 English store draft

Prepared locally; this has not replaced the public version 3 draft. Final billing configuration and release approval remain pending.

**Title:** LifeTalea - Family Stories & Voice Journal

**Short description:** Keep family stories, photographs and voice recordings in a private journal. Organize memories, create keepsakes, and review on-device transcript drafts.

**Long description:**

Keep the moments. Remember the voices.

Give your family stories a place on your Fire tablet. Begin with a photograph, a few written lines, or someone telling a story in their own words. Save stories, photographs and recordings together. Add tags, an optional place and a date as precisely as you know it.

Group memories into albums, mark favorites and explore a family timeline. Search stories, tags and saved transcripts. Gather memories into scrapbook drafts and add captions.

Create an on-device transcript draft from a saved recording. Choose its spoken language and confirm local processing, then review and correct the words before appending them to the saved transcript. No audio is uploaded for transcription. Recordings up to ten minutes can be processed; processing time and accuracy vary. Original audio and manual transcript editing remain available.

LifeTalea requires no account. Your journal is stored on your device. Create a local backup and choose whether it includes photographs and recordings. Command recognition and spoken playback depend on installed speech services; touch and text alternatives remain available.

Premium copy must be finalized after public Amazon billing verification. No cloud sync or universal speech accuracy is claimed.
