LifeTalea 0.1.0, version code 6. No LifeTalea login is required. Use fictional content only. Amazon Test 1 version 6 was installed in-place on a KFTRWI Fire tablet. Its Amazon signature was verified; three DEX files, 383 resource entries, both transcription libraries and model match the uploaded payload. Runtime results are scoped below; public release is not yet approved.

Create written memories with different dates and titles. Add a photograph or a short recording. Combine text, year, album, favourites, media and transcript filters, then clear them. Choose recent, event-date or title sorting. Title sorting persisted through a process restart in the tested build; original recent ordering was restored.

Settings provides 22 interface locales and a device-language option. Arabic navigation mirrors. The owner authorised AI editorial review; no native-speaker certification is claimed. Voice commands support English only and require an installed recognition service. Spoken playback depends on installed voices. Touch and text alternatives remain available.

App lock is optional and off by default. An existing device screen lock is required. Enable and disable it using the operating system's credential screen. Leaving and returning must require Unlock; canceling authentication must leave it locked. The owner completed this sequence on the tablet. The blank Recent-apps preview and return gate were directly observed. LifeTalea does not collect the credential. This is a screen gate, not database encryption; exported files remain separate. Trusted debugging capture paths may still display the foreground app.

Free includes Forest. Rose and Paper require Premium. A Free account selecting Rose opened the paywall with GBP4.99 one-time pricing. Restore remained Free; opening the Amazon confirmation and canceling before Verify and Pay remained Free. No new payment was made. Use Amazon review/test billing accounts; do not spend real money for this QA pass. Active-Premium palette rendering and additional entitlement-recovery cases remain separate checks.

Saved-recording transcription runs on device. Choose the spoken language, confirm processing, review/edit the draft, then append. Preserve original audio and existing text. Invalid audio must allow manual entry. The short English fixture does not qualify accuracy for every language or accent. Processing supports recordings up to ten minutes and may be slow.

Optional reminders are off by default. The tested registration used a one-shot 30-minute window around 20:00 local time, and disabling removed it. Device power management may delay delivery. No story content is included. Actual timed/reboot/time-zone delivery and Android 13 permission branches need separate verification.

Backups currently have 32 MiB archive and 16 MiB attachment limits, with a conservative creation reserve. Encrypted restore of an isolated 1,000-memory fixture preserved selected photos, binary audio fixtures, albums, links and scrapbook content after database reopen. Duplicate restore and interrupted input did not lose records. This is not an unlimited-size or process-death recovery claim.

Settings links to the privacy notice. Purchase verification sends Amazon app-specific user and receipt identifiers to LifeTalea's Supabase-hosted verifier and Amazon; it does not send stories, photos or recordings. Speech-service providers and user-selected export destinations have their own processing policies.
