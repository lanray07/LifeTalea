# Amazon Appstore listing — English (U.S.)

## Title

LifeTalea - Family Stories & Voice Journal

## Short description

Keep family stories, photos, and voice recordings together in a private journal. Organize memories in albums, browse a timeline, and create keepsakes.

## Long description

Keep the moments. Remember the voices.

LifeTalea gives your family stories a place on your Fire tablet. Start with a photo, a few lines of writing, or the sound of someone telling a story in their own words.

Save stories, photos, and voice recordings together. Add tags and an optional place. Record a date as precisely as you know it, from a year to a specific day, or leave it unknown.

Group memories into albums, mark favorites, and browse your family's timeline. Search stories, tags, and transcripts you enter yourself. Gather selected memories into a scrapbook draft and add your own captions.

Your journal is stored on your device. LifeTalea requires no account. Create a local backup and choose whether to include photos and recordings. Voice commands and spoken playback depend on the speech services installed on your device. Touch and text controls are available throughout the app.
