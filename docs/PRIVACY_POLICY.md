# LifeTalea development privacy notice

Publisher: O.Bankole. Contact: banksmi@mail.com. Updated draft: 10 November 2026. Source: https://github.com/lanray07/LifeTalea.

LifeTalea is in development. This notice describes current implementation and needs legal review against the final release. It is not a claim of compliance certification.

Memories, transcripts, tags, manually entered locations, imported photographs, audio and scrapbook drafts are stored in private application storage on your device. No LifeTalea account is required. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs. The app does not collect your location automatically. Android automatic app backup is disabled.

Candidate version 6 adds an optional app lock, off by default. It asks Android to confirm your existing device credential when enabling, disabling or unlocking. LifeTalea does not collect or store that credential. Only the enabled preference is stored locally. While enabled, the app requests screenshot/recents protection and requires unlocking after backgrounding. This screen lock does not encrypt the local database or protect separately exported files.

Microphone access is requested for user-initiated recording or push-to-talk. Saved stories remain local. LifeTalea does not retain raw audio commands. Your installed speech-recognition service may process speech externally and may need internet. Review that service's privacy terms before choosing voice commands. Text and touch controls are available instead. Spoken playback uses your installed TextToSpeech engine; its behaviour depends on that service.

The Amazon Appstore SDK may contact Amazon when the app opens to initialise licensing and refresh account/product information, as well as during a purchase or restore. Amazon purchasing uses Amazon account/product/receipt information to determine Premium access. A receipt verifier is deployed on Supabase. Amazon Test 1 and the configured public candidate use that verifier; an unconfigured standard build cannot initiate purchases. When configured, LifeTalea sends the Amazon app-specific user ID and receipt ID over HTTPS to its private verifier, which checks them with Amazon and returns a signed, expiring entitlement. No memories, recordings or photos are accepted by that service. The app caches the signed receipt assertion in private storage. The verifier has no customer-content database; the Supabase adapter stores only a shared quota counter and timestamp, without customer/receipt/IP identifiers. Supabase and its infrastructure may process request and connection metadata, including IP addresses, headers and invocation records that can contain receipt identifiers. The application handler does not intentionally log request bodies or credentials; this does not disable hosting-platform logs. Final logging, retention, regional processing and provider disclosures still require assessment before public launch. Amazon retains its transaction records under its own policies. Memories and media are not sent as part of purchasing.

Amazon Test 1 version 4 and later candidates offer on-device transcription of saved recordings using a bundled Whisper model. You select the recording language and confirm processing. Neither the recording nor the generated text is uploaded to a transcription provider. The generated text is an editable draft; it is added to your local memory only when you choose to save it. The original recording is preserved. Canceling or leaving the app stops processing. Recognition can make mistakes and requires your review. Cloud sync is not configured.

When you export a PDF or backup you choose the destination through Android's document picker. If that destination is a cloud provider, that provider handles the selected export under its policies. Unencrypted archives contain readable personal content. Optional passphrase encryption protects selected archives, not the separately stored local database. Keep passphrases and backup copies safe. No end-to-end encryption claim is made.

Delete individual content in the app, or use Settings → Delete all local data to remove the local library. Uninstalling also removes private local app data. Exported files remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. Deleting local memories does not remove Amazon transaction records or any file held by another person/provider.

There is no public feed or automatic publication. Obtain permission from people whose voices, photographs or stories you preserve. When contacting support, avoid sending private memories, recordings, backups or passphrases. The marketing preview has no contact submission form and no analytics integration; hosting providers may maintain their own access logs once deployed.

The Supabase project's base region is Frankfurt, Germany. Receipt verification uses Edge Functions that may execute in other regions according to request routing; a base region is not a promise that all processing stays in that region. Amazon and infrastructure providers may also process information in other countries. Actual provider retention and international-transfer arrangements still require the publisher's assessment before public release.

Provider references: [Supabase function logging](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/logging), [Supabase regional execution](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/regional-invocation), [Supabase privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy), [Amazon privacy notice](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ). Contact the publisher to ask about processing of your purchase identifiers; do not include passwords or private recordings.

Optional reminders in candidate version 5 are off by default. When enabled, an inexact daily alarm displays a generic local prompt around 20:00 local time; device power settings can delay delivery. No story text, photo, voice recording or purchase identifier appears in this notification. Android 13 and later require notification permission when enabling it. Turning reminders off cancels the scheduled alarm and displayed reminder. The preference is stored locally, and boot/time-zone broadcasts restore the selected schedule without contacting a server. No notification delivery telemetry is collected by LifeTalea.
